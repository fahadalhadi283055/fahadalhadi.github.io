Gravity Light – Design and Validation of a Sustainable Energy Device

Designed, prototyped, and experimentally validated a gravity-powered lighting system that converts gravitational potential energy into electrical energy using a compact compound planetary gear train and DC motor generator.

Mechanical Design Planetary Gear Train Additive Manufacturing Experimental Validation

Engineering Drawing

Complete gear train assembly drawing including part list, section views, and manufacturing-ready details.

CAD Assembly Model

Parametric CAD assembly of the compound planetary gear system showing sun, planet, ring gears, shafts, spacers, and structural side plates.

Physical Prototype (3D Printed)

Fully assembled prototype manufactured using FDM 3D printing (PLA), validating manufacturability, assembly sequence, and real-world gear meshing behavior.

Experimental Validation Setup

Experimental test rig used to measure dropping speed, generated voltage, and rotational speed under gravitational load conditions.

Key Engineering Outcomes

  • Achieved extreme speed amplification using a compact compound planetary gear train (~2700:1).
  • Validated PLA gear strength using Lewis bending stress theory.
  • Successfully demonstrated gravity-driven motor operation and LED illumination.
  • Identified transmission losses and proposed design improvements for runtime optimization.

Single-Column Screw Press – Design, Manufacturing & Validation

Designed a compact single-column screw press focusing on manufacturability, structural integrity, and safe load transfer. The project covers casting-oriented design, post-machining features, full assembly, and static structural validation using FEA.

Machine Design Casting & Machining CAD Assembly Static Structural FEA

Final Assembly

Complete press assembly showing the screw mechanism, column support, base plate, and load transmission path.

Cast Part Design

Primary load-bearing component designed for casting, incorporating fillets, draft angles, and uniform wall thickness to minimize stress concentrations.

Machined Part

Post-casting machined features including mounting faces, bore alignment, and threaded interfaces to ensure assembly accuracy.

Static Structural Analysis – Safety Factor

Factor of safety plot under applied load conditions showing a minimum safety factor greater than 1.4 at critical regions.

Static Structural Analysis – Stress (von Mises)

von Mises stress distribution highlighting peak stresses at geometric transitions while remaining within allowable material limits.

Key Engineering Outcomes

  • Designed geometry suitable for casting followed by precision machining.
  • Ensured controlled load transfer through the column and base plate.
  • Validated structural safety with minimum safety factor ≈ 1.47.
  • Identified stress concentration zones and addressed them via fillets and geometry refinement.
